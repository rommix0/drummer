drum patterns to 32-bit unsigned int data
with pattern-final marker bit (32th bit)

bits 1-28 (little endian)

for each 2-bit instrument, 00 is silent, 01 is hit, and 10 is flam

   1 - AC                 (accent)
   3 - CY.R               (ride cymbal)
   5 - CY.C               (crash cymbal)
   7 - CH                 (closed hi-hat)
   9 - OH                 (open hi-hat)
  11 - HT                 (high tom)
  13 - MT                 (mid tom)
  15 - SD                 (snare)
  17 - RS                 (rimshot)
  19 - LT                 (low tom)
  21 - CPS                (hand clap)
  23 - TMB                (tambourine)
  25 - CB                 (cowbell)
  27 - BD                 (bass/kick)
  32 - pattern-final
